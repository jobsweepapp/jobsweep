Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

JobSweep stores your job-search data in your own personal iCloud account. We do not operate a server that stores your job data, and we cannot access it.

We do not sell or share your personal information with anyone, for any purpose. We have nothing to sell: your data lives on your device and in your own iCloud account, not on servers we control.

The app requires no account or sign-in. It uses the Apple ID already signed into your device.

If you used a previous version of JobSweep, a one-time, optional import can bring that data into iCloud. This uses a legacy account system that is being phased out. Details about your legacy account, or deletion of it, are available on request. We will delete all remaining legacy data once users have had a reasonable opportunity to migrate, with advance notice in the app before that happens.

We collect no analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data.

If you contact us for support, you choose what information to share, including optional diagnostic details (app version, device model). We do not request your job data, and any support conversation is deleted within 12 months of resolution.

Questions: jobsweepapp@gmail.com
